# Asset attribution

CARLA scene, road, vehicle and derived browser assets originate from the CARLA simulator contributors, https://github.com/carla-simulator/carla and https://bitbucket.org/carla-simulator/carla-content . The pinned source-content commit is `639d6eff5aae672da4219e9ace4a2ee891367548`. These assets are provided under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; see CARLA-ASSET-LICENSE.txt and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

CARLab modifications by Croquembouche include cleaned Town10 scene placement, parking annotations, controllable vehicle variants, Lincoln interior/occupant authoring, traffic signal materials, browser geometry/texture conversions and simulation recordings. These are modified/derived assets, not unchanged upstream exports. Original source licenses and notices remain applicable.

Unreal Engine source patches and engine diagnostic backups are retained separately in the restricted UE5_CARLab_Source repository. They are not part of this public distribution.
