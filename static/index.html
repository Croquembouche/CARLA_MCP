Routing help

Destinations can change while running, including during recording. Select an actor, then click Destination and pick its new goal. Choosing a point from the destination dropdown applies it immediately, including while paused. A new destination replaces the active route without waiting for arrival. Press Run to move when paused. The route status confirms the change. If a vehicle has entered a junction, it completes that movement and follows a new route from the exit. External planners receive the updated goal through the API. Pause before spawning or changing sensors. Background vehicles and ego vehicles can select a road point or a parking bay. With Traffic Manager, parking destinations use reverse entry: the vehicle pulls past the bay, stops, then backs in at low speed. The planner can pull forward to set up the maneuver and always stops before changing direction. Traffic Manager handles the road route. Occupied, undersized or obstructed destinations are rejected. External ego planners receive the exact parking position, heading and bay ID; they retain responsibility for driving the maneuver. Road destination pins snap to drivable lanes. Pedestrians snap to blue sidewalks or sampled walk points. Pedestrian routes come from CARLA navigation. Unreachable destinations are rejected. Set the arrival radius before applying a destination.

External planner connection

ROS 2 domain 42. Topics: /carla/ego_ID/…, /clock, /tf. Controls older than one wall-clock second apply the brake.
